  1. Share your business and project

    Email your current website link if you have one, what your business does, what you need built or fixed, any required content or features, and examples you want considered.

  2. Receive a scope and project agreement

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which website package is right for me?

The Template Launch is for businesses that want an existing Immortal Tofu layout customized with their content and branding. The Custom One-Page Website provides a new layout built around one primary business goal. The Custom Business Website is for multiple pages, unique layouts, analytics, integrations, or more involved requirements.

I will confirm the appropriate package during the scope review before you receive an invoice.

How is a Custom Business Website priced?

Custom Business Websites begin at $800. The starting package includes up to 3 pages. Additional standard pages begin at $125 each. Final pricing depends on page layouts, content needs, analytics, integrations, and launch support.

You receive a written scope and confirmed price before work begins. Any additions requested later are quoted and approved separately.

Is SEO included?

Every new website includes an SEO-ready foundation with semantic HTML, logical headings, page titles and descriptions, descriptive image text, mobile responsiveness, and performance-minded code.

This helps search engines understand the website, but no specific search position or ranking can be guaranteed.

Can you fix my existing website?

Yes. Website Fix & Polish begins at $100 and may include mobile-layout problems, spacing, overflow, navigation, buttons, readability, or other HTML/CSS issues.

Send the live website link and a description of the problem. Broader mobile-responsiveness or multi-page repairs are priced after review.

Why use custom HTML/CSS instead of WordPress or another builder?

Website builders and content-management systems can be useful for frequent self-publishing, advanced e-commerce, or complex content needs. They may also introduce plugins, themes, databases, dashboards, and recurring updates that require additional maintenance.

For straightforward business websites, custom HTML/CSS offers a lighter structure with fewer moving parts, fewer compatibility conflicts, and less unnecessary code. I recommend the simplest platform that genuinely fits the project.

Do you offer hosting and ongoing updates?

Yes. Monthly care is optional and begins after the website build is completed and fully paid. Care plans provide managed hosting, technical support, routine checks, and a defined amount of update time.

Care plans begin with a three-month commitment and continue month to month afterward. Clients who do not need ongoing care can receive a standard website handoff with no required monthly payment.

How do payments and ownership work?

After the scope and Project Agreement are approved, you receive a Stripe invoice. Small repairs are generally paid upfront. Website builds may be paid upfront or split into an initial and final payment, while larger projects may use milestones.

The client owns the completed website after the agreed balance is paid. If you already own a domain, I can connect it. If you prefer a managed setup, I can handle the domain registration and technical setup and arrange transfer when needed.

What do I need to provide?

Provide the core facts about your business, required content, services, contact information, logo, brand colors, available images, and any examples you want considered.

I organize the page structure and can help shape or lightly refine the wording within the approved scope. You review and approve all final content before launch. Extensive copywriting, research, or brand strategy may require a separate quote.
